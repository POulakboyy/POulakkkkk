Privacy policy

Draft dated 4 November 2026, published before the app's release. It will be reviewed by a legal professional and completed (data controller, contact details) before the first public version.

In short

  • Your tasks, events, notes and canvases are stored and processed on your device.
  • You can use POuxis without an account.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Syncing between devices is optional and end-to-end encrypted.

Sync

If you turn it on, content is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before it's sent to a relay. The relay holds no decryption key and can't read your data. To route the encrypted envelopes, it sees:

  • the ID of the synced space;
  • a random device ID, unrelated to any advertising identifier;
  • a logical clock that orders changes;
  • the approximate size of each envelope, rounded to limit what it reveals;
  • the space's access token.

The relay included in the repository doesn't log IP addresses. A host or proxy in front of it might, so check before using a third-party relay. You can also run your own.

This website

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no third-party resources (fonts, scripts or images). The site's host may keep technical connection logs.

Questions

Until there's a dedicated contact address, please ask in the GitHub repository's issues. For security matters, follow the repository's reporting process instead of opening a public issue.

## Premier commentaire du maker (EN)

> Hi Product Hunt,
>
> I'm the maker of POuxis. I make videos about time and attention on my channel, K13 Studios,
> and I kept running into the same problem: seven apps for one day, each one with its own red
> badges and its own way of telling me I wasn't doing enough.
>
> So I'm building one app for the calendar, tasks and ideas, around a single loop: capture, plan,
> focus, review.
>
> A few things that matter to me:
>
> - **Local-first.** Your data lives on your device. The app opens and works without a network.
> - **Encrypted sync, optional.** Devices sync through a relay that only sees encrypted
>   envelopes. You can self-host it.
> - **Open source.** The domain engine is plain TypeScript with no runtime dependencies; the
>   apps run on Tauri 2 for macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android.
> - **Honest about research.** Some features are inspired by published studies (attention
>   residue, broken streaks). Each one links its source and its limits in a public document. If
>   the evidence is thin, we say so.
>
> What it doesn't do yet: no AI, no team features, and a lot of the original 100-idea brief is
> still on the roadmap, on purpose.
>
> I'd love your honest feedback, especially on what feels confusing in the first five minutes.
> I'll be here all day to answer questions.

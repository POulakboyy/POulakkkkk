## Description

<!-- field: apple.description -->

```text
POuxis brings your calendar, your tasks and your ideas together in one calm space. Your data stays on your device: no account to create, no ads, and the app works offline.

Plan without effort
• Type the way you talk: "Project meeting next Tuesday 2-4pm" becomes a dated event.
• POuxis fits your tasks into the open slots of your day, based on how long they should take.
• A task runs over? The rest of the day shifts, while fixed appointments stay put.
• Short transition breaks slot in between back-to-back meetings.
• Your long work blocks are protected: POuxis warns you before a meeting splits them up.

Make progress, one thing at a time
• Focus mode shows a single task, large and alone.
• A Pomodoro timer on each task records the time you actually spent.
• A daily cap on priorities keeps your day realistic.
• A task that depends on another only shows up once the first one is done.
• Urgent-important matrix and Kanban boards, generated from your lists.
• Precise repeats, such as "the 3rd working Tuesday of every month".

Think freely
• An endless Canvas for mind maps and org charts, with automatic layout.
• Quick capture to jot down an idea in seconds, filed in your inbox afterwards.
• The serendipity engine pairs two unrelated notes to spark new connections.
• Random creative constraints to restart a stalled project.
• A sandbox for throwaway drafts, kept out of search.

Understand your rhythm
• Insights spots your most productive hours from your past weeks.
• It compares time spent organizing with time spent making things.
• Every week, a summary of your progress and of the blockers that keep coming back.

Private by design
• Local-first: your data is stored and processed on your device.
• Optional sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac, end-to-end encrypted: the relay server never sees your content.
• Encrypted vaults for sensitive documents.
• A full export of your data, at any time, in an open format.
• Open source code that anyone can review.

Built for everyone
• Instant search across all your notes, tasks and events.
• Full keyboard navigation on iPad, shortcuts, and the Reduce Motion setting respected.
• Available in English and French.

Questions or suggestions? Contact details are on our support page.
```

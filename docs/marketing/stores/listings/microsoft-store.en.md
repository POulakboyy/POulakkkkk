## Description

<!-- field: ms.description -->

```text
POuxis brings your calendar, your tasks and your ideas together in one calm, keyboard-first space. Your data stays on your PC: no account to create, no ads, and the app works offline.

Capture without leaving what you're doing
A global keyboard shortcut opens a capture line over any app. Type the way you talk: "Project meeting next Tuesday 2-4pm" becomes a dated event. The command palette reaches every part of the app without leaving the keyboard.

Plan without effort
POuxis fits your tasks into the open slots of your day, based on how long they should take. When a task runs over, the rest of the day shifts while fixed appointments stay put. Short transition breaks slot in between back-to-back meetings, and your long work blocks are protected: POuxis warns you before a meeting splits them up.

Make progress, one thing at a time
Focus mode shows a single task, full screen. A Pomodoro timer on each task records the time you actually spent, and a daily cap on priorities keeps your day realistic. Urgent-important matrix and Kanban boards are generated from your lists.

Think big
The endless Canvas holds your mind maps and org charts, with automatic layout, by mouse, touchpad or pen. The serendipity engine pairs two unrelated notes to spark new connections, and random creative constraints restart a stalled project.

Understand your rhythm
Insights spots your most productive hours and compares time spent organizing with time spent making things. Every week, it sums up your progress and the blockers that keep coming back.

Private by design
Local-first: your data is stored and processed on your PC. Sync with your other devices is optional and end-to-end encrypted: the relay server never sees your content. Encrypted vaults protect sensitive documents, and a full export of your data is available at any time, in an open format. The source code is open for anyone to review.

Full keyboard navigation, the Windows reduced animation setting respected, available in English and French.
```
